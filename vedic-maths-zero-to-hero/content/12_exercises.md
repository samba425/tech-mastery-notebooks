## Chapter 51: Topic-wise Exercises, Drills and Tests

:::note How to use this bank
- **Topic sections A–H** each have Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced blocks. Finish the Beginner block before moving on.
- **Mixed drills (I)** combine topics, which is how real exams feel.
- **Timed tests (J):** 20 questions in 8 minutes. Write only the answer. Record your score and time in the tracker.
- **Challenge questions (K)** stretch the strongest methods in the book.
- Every answer has a worked solution in the answer key. Read the solution even when you were right; you may find a faster route.
:::

:::tip Marking yourself
Tick correct answers, circle mistakes, and write the **reason** for each mistake (concept, careless, slow). Repeat only the circled questions a week later.
:::
