# Part XIV — Learning Roadmaps

Choose the roadmap that fits you. Every roadmap uses the chapters of this book in a sensible order and has checkpoints, so you know when to move on.

:::warn No guarantees
These plans describe what regular practice typically achieves. Results depend on your starting level, consistency and the quality of your practice. No plan can guarantee a score or a selection.
:::

## Chapter 53: Five Roadmaps

### 53.1 Roadmap A — Complete beginner (adult)

[[flow: Part I foundations | Make-a-ten and complements (Ch 5–7) | Tables and ×11, ×5 (Ch 8) | Nikhilam (Ch 9) | Urdhva (Ch 10) | Squares (Ch 16) | Percentages (Ch 26)]]

**Goal:** comfortable mental arithmetic for daily life and as a base for exams. **Time:** 20–30 minutes a day for 60 days.

### 53.2 Roadmap B — School student (ages 11–16)

Foundations → mental add/sub → multiplication methods → squares/roots → algebra (Part VII) → fractions and percentages. Use the methods to **check** homework, not to skip showing working where the teacher requires it.

### 53.3 Roadmap C — Government-exam aspirant

Foundations review (1 week) → calculation speed (Parts III–VI, 3 weeks) → arithmetic aptitude (Part VIII, 4 weeks) → exam strategy and DI (Part XI) → practice sets and mocks (Part XIII). Follow the 90-day plan below.

### 53.4 Roadmap D — Parent teaching a child

Read Part X first. Follow the level that matches the child's age, with 10–15 minutes a day. Use the Children's Worksheets PDF for printable practice.

### 53.5 Roadmap E — Advanced learner

Duplex squaring, square roots by the long-division method, Paravartya and Nikhilam division, recurring decimals, vinculum, polynomial multiplication and the algebra sutras. Use the Advanced practice sets (61–70).

## Chapter 54: 30, 60 and 90-Day Plans

### 54.1 30-day plan — calculation speed

| Week | Daily focus (25 min) | Checkpoint at the end of the week |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Complements, add/sub left to right, subtraction from powers of 10 | 20 additions of 3-digit numbers in 5 minutes with 90% accuracy |
| 2 | ×11, ×5/25/125, ×9s, Nikhilam near 10 and 100 | 15 near-base products in 5 minutes |
| 3 | Urdhva 2×2, squares ending in 5, near-base squares | any 2-digit × 2-digit in under 20 seconds |
| 4 | Division by 9, 5/25/125, fraction–percentage chart | Timed tests 1 and 2 of Part XII with ≥ 16/20 |

### 54.2 60-day plan — calculation plus aptitude

- **Days 1–30:** the 30-day plan above.
- **Days 31–40:** ratio, averages, percentages, profit and loss (Ch 25–27).
- **Days 41–50:** interest, work, pipes, speed, trains, boats (Ch 28–30).
- **Days 51–60:** alligation, ages, number system, probability, mensuration (Ch 31–35), plus one practice set each day.

### 54.3 90-day plan — government exams

| Phase | Days | Focus | Weekly mock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 1–15 | Parts I–III; tables to 20; squares to 30; cubes to 15 | — |
| Speed | 16–35 | Parts IV–VI; 1 timed test every day | Timed test from Part XII |
| Aptitude | 36–65 | Part VIII, two chapters a week, plus practice sets | 1 sectional mock |
| Exam skills | 66–80 | Part XI; DI and approximation every day | 2 sectional mocks |
| Revision | 81–90 | Part XV formula sheets; repeat circled mistakes | 3 full mocks and their analysis |

## Chapter 55: Daily Routines and Weekly Revision

### 55.1 A 30-minute daily routine

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Warm-up: squares, cubes, tables or bonds (alternate days) |
| 5–15 | Learn or revise one technique from the book |
| 15–25 | 10–15 practice questions on that technique |
| 25–30 | Note mistakes in the tracker; read one recap box |

### 55.2 Weekly revision (Sunday, 45 minutes)

1. Re-do every circled mistake from the week.
2. Take one timed test or practice set.
3. Read the recap boxes of the chapters you studied.
4. Update the tracker: accuracy and average time per topic.
